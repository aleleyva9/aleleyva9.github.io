I am a political scientist and researcher with experience in public policy, international security and media analysis.

At the moment I am focusing entirely on AI governance. I convene senior experts in roundtable discussions on human control over advanced AI, I lead an advocacy campaign and coalition open letter on AI-enabled autonomous weapons, and I research how countries outside the AI frontier can strengthen their institutions and their bargaining position with foreign AI providers.

Before that, I worked at Mexico's Federal Electoral Court advising a magistrate on complex cases and communicating judicial decisions to the public. At Meta I worked on a crisis response team addressing media scrutiny and reputational issues involving AI and data in Europe. At Signal AI I led client-facing research on risk and reputation and acted as the bridge between clients and the engineering teams building their tools.

I hold an MSc in Security, Peacebuilding and Diplomacy from Loughborough University and a BA in Political Science from ITAM.

Areas of inquiry

Middle Powers and Institutional Preparedness

  • How countries outside the frontier can combine complementary sources of leverage over frontier AI developers into credible safeguards
  • Mapping how national institutions recognise and respond to AI risks, with a risk map of Mexico's institutional agenda on AI
  • Exposure to foreign AI systems and the bargaining position of states in the AI supply chain

Post-Control Scenarios

  • What gives humans claims, standing and bargaining power once advanced AI can no longer be fully controlled
  • How governments and organisations can prepare for AI systems that become difficult to control
  • Convening expert roundtables across governance, economics, alignment and artificial minds

Autonomous Weapons

  • Meaningful human control over the use of force, a human decision for every use of force against a person
  • AI-enabled autonomous weapons in civilian and law-enforcement contexts
  • Coalition building and advocacy campaigns for state-level policy
